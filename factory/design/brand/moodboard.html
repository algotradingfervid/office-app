Pick a palette for Placet

Same mark, type and layout; only the colours change. The "Cancel" button shows the keyboard focus ring. Every text colour passes WCAG AA (4.5:1) on its ground; statuses carry a word and a symbol as well as a colour.

A · Ledger green & brass

Bottle-green ledger bindings and brass fittings of an old office register. Green is the colour of "yes"; brass is a quiet warm second note.

Approved uses the brand green itself (the product is about yes); pending is amber, rejected is brick. Each status also has its word and an icon shape, so colour is never the only signal.

B · Petrol & sand

Deep petrol ink (blue-green, like old fountain-pen ink) on sand-coloured paper. Calm and technical without being navy.

Accent (petrol) and approved (green) are different hues, so buttons never look like statuses.

C · Graphite & marigold

Charcoal graphite for structure and buttons, with marigold (the colour of Indian celebration garlands) as the single warm highlight.

Marigold is a fill only (ink text on it, 7.34:1), never text. Pending uses a brown-orange, not marigold, so a highlight never reads as a warning.
