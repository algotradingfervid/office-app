# Voice — Stamp-pad

Placet talks like the one colleague who keeps the register and is fun to deal with: quick, warm,
exact about numbers. The playfulness is in the rhythm of short sentences, never in the facts.

**Adjectives:** brisk, warm, exact.

| We say | We don't say |
|---|---|
| "Stamped. 2 days of CL approved." | "Your leave request has been successfully processed!" |
| "CL runs 2 days max in a row." | "Error: Validation failed for field leave_days." |
| "Ravi has it now." | "Your request has been forwarded to the next approver in the workflow." |

Rules: numbers are always exact and typed (`2 days`, `4 → 2`). No exclamation marks on errors.
Name the person, not the role, when we know them. Never joke about someone's leave reason.

## Five real strings
- **Welcome:** "Morning, Asha. 4 days of CL in the bank."
- **Empty state:** "Nothing waiting on you. Desk's clear."
- **Error:** "CL runs 2 days max in a row. Shorten it, or switch to EL."
- **Success toast:** "Stamped. 2 days of CL approved by Ravi."
- **Destructive confirm:** "Cancel this leave? 2 days go back to your CL. The history keeps a note of it." Buttons: "Cancel leave" / "Keep it".
