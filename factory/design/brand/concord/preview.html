The mark

Two squares, two people: a large solid one and a smaller outlined one on its corner. Where they meet, the outline crosses the solid in the second blue and leaves a shared block: one decision. Flat, two blues, no gradients; the one-colour version cuts through instead.

Palette

Paper, white cards, ink text and one ink blue. Status colours appear on status text, message rules and the one destructive button. Every text colour passes WCAG AA (at least 4.5:1) on its ground; the ratios are in palette.md.
